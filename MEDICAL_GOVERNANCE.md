# Medical and public-health governance

TERRA07 is a public-safety communication system, not a diagnostic medical model.

## Allowed

- Fixed, versioned prevention and emergency guidance aligned with authoritative sources.
- Earlier prompts for broad user groups that may need extra heat protection.
- Guidance selection based on voluntary profile answers.
- A strict fluid-restriction override.
- Directing medicine-related questions to a doctor or pharmacist.
- Emergency escalation for possible heatstroke signs.

## Not allowed

- Predicting the exact temperature at which a person will become ill.
- Diagnosing heat exhaustion or heatstroke.
- Prescribing water, salt, medicine or treatment doses.
- Advising a user to stop/change medicine.
- Rewriting emergency instructions dynamically with a generative model.
- Weakening or hiding an official warning because of personal history.

## Required before public clinical claims

- Named licensed physician/public-health reviewer for each content record.
- Review date, next-review date and source version.
- Separate emergency-language review.
- Human-reviewed translations and back-translation for safety-critical text.
- Accessibility review, screen-reader support and colour-independent warning states.
- Audit trail of which approved content version was shown and why.
- Privacy/security review for profile, location and trusted-contact data.
